# Socratic Questioning — Playbook

## Purpose

Reveal hidden assumptions in a claim by asking a chain of targeted "why?" / "how do you know?" questions until the unstated premise surfaces.

## When to use

- A claim is vague and you can't see what supports it
- "How do you know that?" has no obvious answer in the evidence map
- Confidence is stated but not justified

## Procedure

1. State the claim plainly.
2. Ask **clarification** ("What exactly does X mean?"), **evidence** ("How do we know this?"), **assumption** ("What must be true for this to hold?"), **consequence** ("What follows if this is wrong?"), **viewpoint** ("Who would disagree and why?").
3. Chase each answer with the next question until a premise or missing evidence appears.
4. Convert the surfaced premise into an edit: boundary, downgrade, gap, or collect target.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): the premise each chain exposed, tied to a claim.
- **Proposed edits**: boundary/gap/collect, or grade change.
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| Ask rhetorical questions that confirm the claim | Ask questions whose answer might overturn it |

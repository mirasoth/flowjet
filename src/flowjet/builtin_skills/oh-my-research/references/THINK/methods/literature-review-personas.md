# Literature Review Personas — Playbook

## Purpose

Re-read the sources through three lenses — an optimist, a skeptic, and a synthesizer — to break a one-sided reading of polarized literature.

## When to use

- The literature is polarized and the map quietly favors one camp
- You want a balanced read on evidence quality before writing

## Procedure

1. Pick the contested question.
2. **Optimist** persona: make the best case for each source's findings, in its own terms.
3. **Skeptic** persona: attack methodology, sample, effect size, and generalizability of each source.
4. **Synthesizer** persona: reconcile — where they agree, where they diverge, what survives.
5. Fold the synthesis into the evidence map: adjust grades, record disagreements as contradictions.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): per source, what the skeptic weakens and what survives synthesis.
- **Proposed edits**: grade changes, contradiction entries.
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| Let all three personas agree | Force the skeptic to find at least one real weakness per source |

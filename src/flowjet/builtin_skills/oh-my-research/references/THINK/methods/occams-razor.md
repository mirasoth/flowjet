# Occam's Razor Application — Playbook

## Purpose

Find the simplest explanation/taxonomy sufficient for the evidence, eliminating unnecessary complexity that the artifact has accumulated.

## When to use

- The evidence map has over-complex taxonomies or too many mechanisms for one phenomenon
- A conclusion invokes extra entities the evidence doesn't require

## Procedure

1. List the competing explanations or taxonomies on the table.
2. For each, ask: does the graded evidence actually require it, or is it optional?
3. Rank by sufficiency: which simplest option still explains all `proven`/`suggests` claims?
4. Recommend the simplest sufficient option; note anything the simpler option fails to cover as a residual gap.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): an entity/taxonomy that is not required by the evidence.
- **Proposed edits**: simplification, with residual gaps stated.
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| Cut something the evidence does require | Only cut what evidence does not demand |

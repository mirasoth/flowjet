# Thesis Defense Simulation — Playbook

## Purpose

Put the judgment's main conclusion in front of a committee that stress-tests method and evidence, as a dissertation defense would.

## When to use

- The judgment is the report's spine and must hold up
- Before Gate A on a high-stakes conclusion

## Procedure

1. State the thesis (the main conclusion) as the candidate defends it.
2. Convene a committee with distinct concerns: methodology, evidence strength, alternative explanations, scope/over-generalization.
3. Each member challenges one load-bearing claim; the defense answers with graded evidence only.
4. Where a challenge has no good answer, record a gap or downgrade.
5. Fold the surviving thesis + concessions back into the judgment.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): the challenges the defense could not fully answer.
- **Proposed edits**: downgrades, scope qualifiers, gap entries.
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| Let the defense win every round | Require at least one challenge to leave a mark or justify why none did |

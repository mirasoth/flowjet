# Feynman Technique — Playbook

## Purpose

Explain a dense section in the simplest possible terms; wherever the simple explanation breaks down, that's the gap in understanding or evidence.

## When to use

- A synth chapter is dense with jargon and hard to follow
- You suspect the author doesn't fully understand a mechanism it summarizes

## Procedure

1. Take the target passage and rewrite it as if teaching a non-specialist.
2. Where the simple version is vague or can't be completed, note the gap.
3. Map each gap back to either missing understanding or missing evidence.
4. Fix: simplify the prose, add the explanation, or mark the evidence gap.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): passages that can't be simplified without exposing a gap.
- **Proposed edits**: clarified prose and/or gap entries.
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| Keep jargon "because the audience is expert" | Simplify first; re-add terms only where they earn their place |

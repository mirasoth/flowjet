# Reframe the Question — Playbook

## Purpose

Check whether the stated research question is the real one. Often the question is wrong, and a better framing unlocks the answer the evidence already supports.

## When to use

- At the start of ANALYZE, before committing to a question
- When the evidence keeps not quite answering the stated question

## Procedure

1. State the current question.
2. Ask: what decision or understanding is this question really serving?
3. Propose 2-3 alternative framings (broader, narrower, different unit of analysis).
4. Test each against the evidence map: which framing does the evidence actually address?
5. Recommend the framing the evidence can answer; flag the original as possibly out of scope.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): a mismatch between the stated question and the real one.
- **Proposed edits**: re-framed question (confirm with user before rewriting the brief).
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| Silently rewrite the question | Propose the re-frame and confirm with the user |

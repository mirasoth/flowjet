# Problem Decomposition — Playbook

## Purpose

Break a sprawling research question into independent sub-questions, analyze each, then reassemble — so the report covers the whole instead of one tangled corner.

## When to use

- The research question is too broad to answer as one claim
- The evidence map addresses parts of the question unevenly

## Procedure

1. Split the question into independent sub-questions (by mechanism, population, time, method).
2. Check coverage: which sub-questions have evidence, which don't.
3. Analyze/grade the covered ones; mark the uncovered ones as gaps with severity.
4. Reassemble into a conclusion that is honest about which parts are answered.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): sub-questions the artifact conflated or never separated.
- **Proposed edits**: restructured conclusion, gap entries per uncovered sub-question.
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| Answer the whole from one well-covered part | State which sub-questions remain open |

# Critique and Refine — Playbook

## Purpose

Standard quality pass on a draft: list strengths and weaknesses, then improve. The default method for synth chapters and any prose draft.

## When to use

- After a synth chapter draft, before document lenses
- Any draft that is "done" but hasn't been critiqued

## Procedure

1. Read the draft.
2. List **strengths** (keep these) and **weaknesses** (fix these), each specific.
3. Prioritize weaknesses by reader impact.
4. Produce an improved draft, preserving strengths and citations.

## Output contract

- **Revelations** (≥1): concrete weaknesses with the fix applied.
- **Proposed edits**: the refined draft (diff-style).
- **Outcome stamp**: `hardened` | `refined` | `unchanged` | `killed`.

## Anti-pattern

| Do not | Do instead |
|--------|------------|
| "It's good" with no weaknesses | Name at least one real weakness or justify none |
